## chg-001: Attach the controlling definition to each flagged clause

- Research questions: RQ1, RQ3
- Sessions: ses-01 (Reviewing senior lawyer, observed task); ses-03 (In-house counsel, observed task); ses-05 (Reviewing senior lawyer, follow up task); ses-06 (In-house counsel, follow up task)
- Problem (prb-01, quality or control failure): A flagged clause reaches the reviewer without the definition that controls it.

**Observed**

- obs-01, observed task: Signed off the issues list as it stood after 14 minutes. Did not open the definitions clause. Accepted the liability-cap item as listed.
- obs-05, observed task: Reached the 20-minute limit without a decision and said the list would have to be redone from the contract. Had found the liability-cap discrepancy but could not tell which definition applied.
- obs-06, past behaviour (interview): On the last review, read the definitions clause first and the liability clauses second, before opening the issues list.

**Interpretation** (Legal engineer (synthetic)): Two reviewers failed the task at the same point, and one reads definitions first by habit. The list item probably needs to carry the controlling definition; whether that alone prevents a wrong sign-off is untested.

- Hypothesis: If a list item shows the definition that controls its clause, reviewers will catch a scope error in the liability cap without re-reading the definitions clause.
- Proposed change: Resolve cross-referenced definitions before a clause is listed and show the controlling definition on the list item.
- Decision: prioritised on 24 Mar 2026, confidence medium (Product lead (synthetic)). A wrong sign-off was observed in one of three task sessions and a failed task in another. Quality and control failures are reviewed first regardless of the size of the accounts that raised them.
- Acceptance criteria (recorded 24 Mar 2026): ac-001-1 On a contract where a definition changes the scope of a later clause, the list item shows that definition. ac-001-2 A reviewer can open the definition from the item without searching the contract.
- Implementation reference: synthetic-tracker/REV-31
- Delivery: released on 26 May 2026, evidence: synthetic-release-notes/2026-05#controlling-definitions
- Outcome: Supported. Both reviewers who failed on TP-A reached an acceptable result on TP-B and opened the definition from the item. The hypothesis is supported for these two cases.
