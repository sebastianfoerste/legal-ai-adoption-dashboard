# Follow-up questions

**Use this when** a session is over and you want to ask the participant something more, or
when a product change has been made and you prepare the questions for the follow-up
session.

Status: draft. No question has been sent or asked. Sending any message to a participant
needs the owner's authorisation, and contact is possible only while the participant's
contact details are still held under [data-handling.md](data-handling.md).

Keep the evidence types apart here as in the session. Log each answer on the participant's
[observation sheet](observation-sheet.md) with its evidence type and research question.

## Set 1: after a round-1 session

Ask these at the close if time allows, or send them within two working days. Send at most
one message and at most four questions.

### `past_behaviour`

| No. | Question | RQ |
|---|---|---|
| FQ1 | After the session, did you remember anything about the last real handoff that you did not mention? What was it? | RQ1, RQ2 |
| FQ2 | Since the session, have you reviewed or handed over a contract issues list in your own work? Without naming the client or matter: what did you do first on that occasion? | RQ1 |

### `observed_task`

None. Evidence of this type comes only from what the moderator sees in a session. A
written answer about the task is a self-report. If a participant comments on the exercise
afterwards, log the comment as `opinion_on_proposal` when it concerns the idea, and as a
note marked "participant's explanation" when it concerns the task.

### `opinion_on_proposal`

| No. | Question | RQ |
|---|---|---|
| FQ3 | Having had time to think about the described handoff note: is there anything you now see differently? | RQ5 |
| FQ4 | Is there information you wanted from the producer in the exercise that the described note does not contain? | RQ4 |

## Set 2: after a product change

Use these in the follow-up session described in
[follow-up-session-plan.md](follow-up-session-plan.md). They replace Part 1 and Part 3 of
the [moderator script](moderator-script.md) for a returning participant. A new participant
gets Part 1 of the round-1 script, then Part 2 with `TP-B` in the changed form, then FC6
and FC7.

### `past_behaviour` (Part 1 of the follow-up session)

| No. | Question | RQ |
|---|---|---|
| FC1 | Tell me about the last time a contract issues list reached you for review since {{round_1_session_date}}. Without naming the client or matter, what did you do from the moment it reached you? | RQ1 |
| FC2 | On that occasion, did you write or type anything of your own? What was it? | RQ2 |
| FC3 | On that occasion, at what point did you open the contract itself, and what sent you there? | RQ2 |

If no such occasion took place, write that down and go to Part 2. Do not ask what the
participant would do.

### `observed_task` (Part 2 of the follow-up session)

The moderator asks no questions during the task. Use the same instruction, think-aloud
reminder, help rule and logging as in round 1. After the task, the same clarifying
question is allowed:

| No. | Question | RQ |
|---|---|---|
| FC4 | At one point you went to {{place_in_document}}. What were you looking for? | RQ3, RQ4 |

Log the answer as `observed_task`, marked "participant's explanation".

### `opinion_on_proposal` (Part 3 of the follow-up session)

| No. | Question | RQ |
|---|---|---|
| FC5 | What, if anything, was different for you in today's exercise compared with the first one? | RQ4 |
| FC6 | Which part of what you were given today did you not use? | RQ4 |
| FC7 | What would you still have to ask the producer before signing off? | RQ4 |

Answers in this part are opinions about the changed handoff and cannot show that the
change works. Only the comparison of the task measures can support that conclusion, within
the limits set out in the follow-up session plan.
