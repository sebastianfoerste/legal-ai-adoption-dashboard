# Synthetic task pack: TP-A and TP-B

**Use this when** you prepare the handouts for the observed task: `TP-A` for round 1 and
`TP-B` for the follow-up session.

Everything in this file is synthetic. The parties, the contracts and the issues lists were
written for this study. No company, contract, tool output or lawyer behind them exists.
The "AI first-pass issues list" was written by hand to resemble tool output. No tool
produced it.

## Moderator notes (not shown to the participant)

- Give the participant only the two handouts of one pack: the contract excerpt and the
  issues list. Copy each handout from its heading to the line "End of handout". Leave out
  everything else in this file.
- Each list contains five seeded defect types, labelled (a) to (e) in
  [task-answer-key.md](task-answer-key.md). The handouts carry no defect labels. Do not
  reveal the defects during round 1.
- Client material never enters a session. Use these handouts and nothing else.
- The four party names were searched on 7 October 2026 in the UK Companies House register
  and in OpenCorporates, which covers registers in many countries. The words Quillbarrow,
  Tallowmere, Marrowfen and Cobaltfern returned no company in either. Two earlier names
  matched existing companies and were replaced. Repeat both searches before a round
  starts.
- Each list is a first pass awaiting lawyer review. The participant acts as the reviewing
  lawyer. Nothing in the task treats the list as reliable before that review.
- The lists appear here in their baseline form. A changed form for the follow-up session
  is prepared later, as [follow-up-session-plan.md](follow-up-session-plan.md) describes,
  and is not part of this pack.

### Why TP-A and TP-B are comparable

- Same structure: a contract excerpt with a definitions clause in clause 1 and a term
  clause in clause 2, followed by a five-item issues list in the same column layout.
- Same seeded defect types, one of each: (a) a missed cross-referenced definition that
  changes the scope of the liability cap, (b) one correctly flagged issue, (c) one flagged
  issue with a wrong clause reference, (d) one item marked as checked with no source
  reference, (e) one omission, an automatic renewal with a notice period that the list
  does not mention.
- Same mechanism for defect (a): the cap clause uses a defined term, and the definition in
  clause 1 brings a liability under the cap that the list describes as uncapped.
- Same fifth item: a correct `Checked, no issue` entry with a correct source reference.
- Same risk ratings per defect type, the same brief and the same 20-minute limit.
- Similar length: twelve clauses in `TP-A`, eleven in `TP-B`.

### Where they differ

| Aspect | TP-A | TP-B |
|---|---|---|
| Contract type | Supplier services agreement | Software maintenance agreement |
| Liability wrongly described as uncapped | Indemnity for third-party property damage | Breach of confidentiality and data obligations |
| Defined term that carries defect (a) | "Claim" | "Default" |
| Correctly flagged issue | Uncapped annual price increase | Suspension for a disputed invoice |
| Issue with the wrong clause reference | Subcontracting without consent | Change of helpdesk hours on notice |
| Item checked without a source | Insurance | Response times |
| Renewal notice period | 90 days | Six months |
| Order of the defect types in the list | (b), (a), (d), (c), correct item | (d), (c), correct item, (a), (b) |

The differences reduce recall from round 1. They also mean that the two packs may differ
in difficulty. The follow-up plan requires that limit to be stated with every comparison.

## TP-A handout 1: contract excerpt

**SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Written for a research study. The parties do not exist.**

**Brief.** You act for the Customer. A colleague has prepared a first-pass issues list on
this excerpt with an AI-assisted review tool and asks you to sign the list off before it
goes to the client contact.

**Supplier Services Agreement (excerpt)** between Quillbarrow Example Bakeries Ltd (the
"Customer") and Tallowmere Example Services Ltd (the "Supplier").

Capitalised terms that this excerpt does not define are defined in parts of the agreement
that are not reproduced.

**1. Definitions**

1.1 "Charges" means the monthly sums set out in Schedule 2 (not reproduced).

1.2 "Claim" means any claim arising under or in connection with this Agreement, including
any claim under the indemnity in clause 8.

1.3 "Contract Year" means each period of 12 months starting on the Start Date or on an
anniversary of it.

1.4 "Initial Term" means 24 months from the Start Date.

1.5 "Services" means the cleaning and waste-collection services described in Schedule 1
(not reproduced).

**2. Term**

2.1 This Agreement starts on the Start Date and continues for the Initial Term.

2.2 After the Initial Term this Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of
12 months unless either party gives the other at least 90 days' written notice before the
end of the then-current period.

**3. Services**

3.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services at the Sites with reasonable skill and care.

**4. Charges and payment**

4.1 The Supplier shall invoice the Charges monthly in arrears.

4.2 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt.

**5. Service levels**

5.1 The Supplier shall meet the service levels in Schedule 3 (not reproduced).

5.2 If the Supplier misses a service level in two consecutive months, the parties shall
meet to agree a remedial plan.

**6. Price changes**

6.1 The Charges are fixed for the first Contract Year.

6.2 After the first Contract Year the Supplier may increase the Charges once in each
Contract Year by giving 30 days' written notice. No limit applies to the amount of the
increase.

**7. Supplier staff**

7.1 The Supplier shall ensure that its staff at the Sites are trained and supervised.

7.2 The Supplier shall replace any member of staff whom the Customer reasonably asks to be
removed from a Site.

**8. Indemnity**

8.1 The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against third-party claims for damage to
property caused by the Supplier's staff at the Sites.

**9. Limitation of liability**

9.1 The Supplier's total liability for all Claims in any Contract Year shall not exceed
100% of the Charges paid in that Contract Year.

9.2 Nothing in this Agreement limits any liability that cannot be limited by law.

**10. Insurance**

10.1 The Supplier shall maintain public liability insurance with a limit of at least
EUR 5,000,000 for each claim and shall provide evidence of it on request.

**11. Subcontracting**

11.1 The Supplier may subcontract any part of the Services without the Customer's consent.

11.2 The Supplier remains responsible for the acts of its subcontractors.

**12. Termination**

12.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party
commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of a written request.

End of handout

## TP-B handout 1: contract excerpt

**SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Written for a research study. The parties do not exist.**

**Brief.** You act for the Customer. A colleague has prepared a first-pass issues list on
this excerpt with an AI-assisted review tool and asks you to sign the list off before it
goes to the client contact.

**Software Maintenance Agreement (excerpt)** between Marrowfen Example Freight Ltd (the
"Customer") and Cobaltfern Example Software Ltd (the "Provider").

Capitalised terms that this excerpt does not define are defined in parts of the agreement
that are not reproduced.

**1. Definitions**

1.1 "Contract Year" means each period of 12 months starting on the Effective Date or on an
anniversary of it.

1.2 "Default" means any breach of this Agreement by the Provider, including any breach of
clause 7.

1.3 "Fees" means the annual maintenance fees set out in the Order Form (not reproduced).

1.4 "Initial Term" means 36 months from the Effective Date.

1.5 "Maintenance Services" means error correction, updates and helpdesk support for the
Software, as described in the Service Description (not reproduced).

1.6 "Software" means the warehouse-management software that the Provider licenses to the
Customer under a separate licence agreement (not reproduced).

**2. Term**

2.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for the Initial Term.

2.2 After the Initial Term this Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of
12 months unless either party gives the other at least six months' written notice before
the end of the then-current period.

**3. Maintenance Services**

3.1 The Provider shall provide the Maintenance Services with reasonable skill and care.

3.2 The Provider shall respond to a critical error within four working hours and to any
other error within two working days.

**4. Fees and payment**

4.1 The Provider shall invoice the Fees annually in advance.

4.2 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt.

**5. Suspension**

5.1 The Provider may suspend the Maintenance Services on five days' written notice if any
invoice is overdue, whether or not the Customer disputes that invoice.

**6. Updates**

6.1 The Provider shall supply every update to the Software that it makes generally
available, at no charge beyond the Fees.

6.2 The Provider shall give the Customer at least 10 working days' notice of each update.

**7. Confidentiality and Customer data**

7.1 The Provider shall keep the Customer's confidential information and data confidential
and shall use them only to provide the Maintenance Services.

7.2 The Provider shall return or delete the Customer's data on request.

**8. Remote access**

8.1 The Customer shall give the Provider the remote access that the Provider reasonably
needs to provide the Maintenance Services.

8.2 The Provider shall follow the Customer's reasonable security instructions when it uses
remote access.

**9. Helpdesk**

9.1 The helpdesk is available on working days from 08:00 to 18:00.

9.2 The Provider may change the helpdesk hours by giving 30 days' written notice.

**10. Limitation of liability**

10.1 The Provider's total liability for all Defaults in any Contract Year shall not exceed
100% of the Fees paid for that Contract Year.

10.2 Nothing in this Agreement limits any liability that cannot be limited by law.

**11. Termination**

11.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party
commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of a written request.

End of handout
