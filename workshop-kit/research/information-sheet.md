## Purpose

We want to understand one step in contract review: the point where a first-pass issues
list, produced with an AI-assisted tool, passes from the lawyer who prepared it to the
lawyer who reviews it and signs it off. We want to learn how that review is done in
practice and where information gets lost on the way. What we learn will inform changes to
a product and a later session that tests whether a change helped.

We study the handoff and the materials. We do not assess you or your legal judgement.

## What happens

You take part in one session with one moderator. A note-taker may join; we tell you
beforehand if so. Nobody else watches.

1. Introduction and consent, about 10 minutes.
2. We ask about the last time you prepared or reviewed a contract issues list that passed
   between two lawyers, about 15 minutes.
3. You review a short contract excerpt and an issues list while the moderator watches and
   you say aloud what you are doing, about 20 minutes.
4. We show you a description of an idea and ask what you think of it, about 10 minutes.
5. Close, about 5 minutes.

## Time needed

60 minutes, `{{session_format}}`. Reading this sheet and the consent form takes about 10
minutes beforehand.

## Synthetic documents only

The contract, the parties and the issues list are made up for this study. Like any
first-pass list, the list may contain mistakes. We explain how the materials were built
once your part in the study, including any follow-up session, is over.

Please do not bring or describe client material. Please do not name your employer, a
client, a colleague or a matter, and do not describe a matter in a way that would identify
it. Your professional confidentiality duties apply as usual. If a name slips out, we do
not write it down.

If you need your employer's permission to take part, please obtain it first. We do not
record who your employer is.

## What we record

- Written notes of what you say and do in the session.
- Your role in general terms, for example "reviewing senior lawyer", and a band for your
  years of experience.
- An audio or video recording (`{{recording_type}}`), only if you agree to it separately on
  the consent form. You can take part without being recorded.
- Your name and contact details, and your signed consent form.

We do not record the name of your employer or of any client.

## How we handle your data

- The notes carry a study code such as `P01`, not your name.
- Your contact details and your consent form are stored apart from the notes. Only the
  research team can connect the code to you.
- We delete your contact details within 30 days of the last session of the study round you
  take part in. If you separately agree to be contacted about a follow-up session, we keep
  them until `{{follow_up_contact_deadline}}` at the latest.
- If a recording is made, we use it only to check the notes and delete it within 14 days
  once the notes are checked.
- We keep the coded notes for 24 months. After that we delete them, unless their further
  use has been approved again.
- Other organisations that handle the data: `{{processors_or_none}}`.
- The basis on which we handle your personal data: `{{legal_basis}}`.

## How we use the results

We combine what we learn from all sessions into findings about the handoff. The findings
describe a small number of sessions and name no person, employer or client.

We use your own words in a report or publication only if you agree to that separately on
the consent form. Such a quotation is attributed by role label only, for example
"in-house counsel". We do not use a quotation that could identify you. Without that
agreement, your words stay inside the research team.

## Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Taking part is your choice. Declining has no consequence. During the session you can skip
a question, pause or stop at any time without giving a reason.

You can withdraw after the session until we combine the findings from all sessions, which
we plan for `{{aggregation_date}}`. If you withdraw before that date, we delete your notes
and any recording. After that date your contribution is part of the combined findings and
cannot be taken out of them.

To withdraw, contact `{{research_contact}}` and give your name. You do not need to give a
reason.
