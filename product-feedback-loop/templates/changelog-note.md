# Changelog note template — closing the loop with customers

Use this to tell customers what shipped from their feedback. Keep it short and specific.

Send it only for a change whose delivery record in `data/evidence-loop.json` is `released`, and say that it helped only when the recorded outcome is `supported`. For every other item give the recorded status. The note is a draft for human review before it is sent.

**Subject:** What we shipped this month from your feedback

Hi {name},

You told us: *{the pain, in the customer's words}.*

We changed: **{the requirement that shipped}.** {one sentence on how to use it.}

Still on our list from your feedback:
- {theme} — {status: in progress / planned}

Thanks for shaping the product.
{your name}
